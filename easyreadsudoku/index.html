Easy Read Sudoku: Large Print

Classic sudoku with big numbers, a large number pad, no game over and hints that explain why. Every puzzle has one answer and can be solved by logic alone – no guessing.

How to play

Fill the board so every row, every column and every 3×3 box has the numbers 1 to 9 once. Tap a square, then tap a number. Tap Notes to write small pencil marks instead.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if I make a mistake? Nothing bad – there is no game over. Tap Undo (free and unlimited) or put in a different number. You choose how mistakes are shown in Settings → Show mistakes: Off (like paper), Rule conflicts only, or Check against the answer. Notes never count as mistakes.

I'm stuck. What do I do? Tap Hint. Step 1 shows where to look and is always free. Step 2 explains the reason, and step 3 can place the number for you. If a number you entered is wrong, the hint points to it first.

The notes are too small. Turn on Settings → Large notes.

The screen is too bright. Choose Settings → Theme → Night, or High contrast.

Will I lose my game if I close the app? No. Your puzzle is saved and you can continue where you left off.

I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads. Open Settings (the sliders icon at the top right of the home screen) and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it. Optional reward videos for extra hints stay available if you want them.

Will my progress come back if I reinstall the app or get a new phone? Yes, with no account needed. On iPhone, progress is kept in your iCloud (same Apple ID, iCloud turned on). On Android, it comes back through your Google backup when backup is turned on.
