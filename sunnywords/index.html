Sunny Words: Large Print

A calm, classic word search with big, clear letters, high contrast mode and a new Daily Puzzle every day.

How to play

Slide your finger across a word, or tap the first letter and then the last letter. Easy puzzles go across and down, Medium adds diagonals, and Hard hides words in every direction.

Frequently asked questions

I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads. Open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open Settings → Privacy choices.

Does the app work offline? Yes. Puzzles work without an internet connection.
