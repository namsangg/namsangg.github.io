Easy Read Mexican Train

Mexican Train dominoes with big numbers, fair computer players, and a free score sheet for your real game night.

How to play

New to the game? Tap Learn in 1 minute on the home screen. Each round starts from a double in the middle. Play matching tiles on your own train or on the Mexican Train. If you can't play, draw a tile; if you still can't, your train opens and anyone may play on it until you play on it again. When a double is played, it must be covered before anything else. The lowest total after all rounds wins.

Frequently asked questions

Does the computer see my tiles? No. Computer players only use what they could see at a real table. After each round, tap Show all hands to see every tile, how many each player drew and passed, and Replay this round to play the same tiles again.

Can I see numbers instead of dots? Yes. Numbers are the default. Switch between numbers and big dots in Settings.

Our group plays different rules. Open House rules to choose how doubles must be covered, what the 0-0 tile counts, whether you may play several tiles on your first turn, and the game length. The same rules apply to the score sheet.

How do I keep score for a real game? Tap Keep score for a real game, add the players, and enter each round's points with the number pad or by tapping the tiles left in a hand. You can fix a round, see the standings, and share the result.

Will I lose my game if I close the app? No. Your game and score sheet are saved. Tap Continue on the home screen.

I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads. Open Settings → Remove ads and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it.

Will my progress come back on a new phone? Yes, with no account needed. On iPhone it is kept in your iCloud (same Apple ID, iCloud on). On Android it comes back through your Google backup when backup is on.
