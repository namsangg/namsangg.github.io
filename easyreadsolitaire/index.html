Easy Read Solitaire

Classic Klondike solitaire with big, easy-to-read cards, tap-to-move, and deals that can always be won.

How to play

Move all 52 cards to the four piles at the top, from Ace up to King in each suit. In the seven columns, stack cards down in alternating colors (for example, a red 6 on a black 7). Only a King can go in an empty column. Tap the deck to turn over new cards.

Just tap a card to move it to the best place. You can also drag it. If a card can't move, the app tells you why.

Frequently asked questions

Can every deal be won? Yes. Every deal was checked by a computer solver before it was added to the game. If you get stuck, use Undo or restart the same deal.

Spades and clubs look alike to me. Turn on Settings → Four-color deck. Diamonds turn blue and clubs turn green.

Cards jump to the top piles by themselves. That is Settings → Auto-move to top piles. It is off unless you turn it on.

Can I turn over 3 cards at a time? Yes. Choose Settings → Turn over → 3 cards. It starts with your next new game.

I'm left-handed. Turn on Settings → Left-handed to put the deck on the left.

Will I lose my game if I close the app? No. Your game is saved and you can continue where you left off.

I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads. Open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it. Optional reward videos for extra hints stay available if you want them.

Will my progress come back if I reinstall the app or get a new phone? Yes, with no account needed. On iPhone, progress is kept in your iCloud (same Apple ID, iCloud turned on). On Android, it comes back through your Google backup when backup is turned on. A "Remove ads" purchase can always be restored from Settings → Restore purchase.
