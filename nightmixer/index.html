Night Mixer: Sleep Sounds

Mix brown noise, rain, ocean waves, fan and more into your own relaxing soundscape, with a gentle sleep timer.

How to use

Tap any sound to start. Tap more sounds to mix them, and drag each slider to set its volume. Use Sleep timer to fade the sounds out and stop. Sounds keep playing with the screen off.

Frequently asked questions

The sound stops when I lock my phone. Make sure the app is not force-closed and that battery saver is not restricting it (Android: Settings → Apps → Night Mixer → Battery → Unrestricted).

How do I unlock premium sounds? Watch a short video to use a premium sound for 24 hours, or buy Remove ads once to unlock all sounds forever.

I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads. Open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it.

Is this a medical device? No. Night Mixer is made for relaxation and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Keep the volume comfortable, especially for babies.
