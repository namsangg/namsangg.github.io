Paint Pour: Color Mix Puzzle

A relaxing tube puzzle with a twist: pour two different primary colors together and they mix into a new color.

How to play

Tap a tube, then tap another tube to pour. Same colors stack. Red + Yellow makes Orange, Yellow + Blue makes Green, and Red + Blue makes Purple. Mixed colors only stack on the same color. Fill every tube with a single color to finish the level.

Frequently asked questions

I mixed the wrong colors. Tap Undo, or tap the restart button at the top.

I have trouble telling the colors apart. Turn on Settings → Color symbols to show a letter on every color.

I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads. Open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open Settings → Privacy choices.
