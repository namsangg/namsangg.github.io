Easy Read Mahjong – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Easy Read Mahjong ("the app") is a mahjong solitaire tile-matching puzzle game. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. Our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may infer your approximate location (for example, country or city) from your IP address to show relevant ads and prevent fraud.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, saved boards, daily puzzle streak and settings are stored on your device. The app does not use the camera or microphone. On iPhone, a copy of this progress is also kept in your own iCloud account (Apple iCloud key-value storage, using the Apple ID you are signed in with), so it can come back after you reinstall the app or move to a new device. On Android, it may be included in your device\'s Google backup if you have backup turned on. We cannot see or access this copy. You can remove it by turning off iCloud for the app in iOS Settings, or by turning off backup on Android.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your advertising identifier, IP address, device model and app interactions. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy choices. On iOS, the app asks for permission before using your advertising identifier for tracking.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used (for example, which levels are completed and whether hints are used) and to fix crashes. This data is collected with pseudonymous identifiers and is not used to identify you personally.

In-app purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The app only remembers on your device (and in the iCloud or Google backup described above) whether ads have been removed, so the purchase can be restored.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
