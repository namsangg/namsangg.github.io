Easy Read Mahjong

A relaxing mahjong solitaire with big, easy-to-read tiles, no timer, and boards that can always be solved.

How to play

Tap two matching tiles to remove them. A tile can be picked only when nothing is on top of it and its left or right side is open. Clear every tile to finish the board.

Tiles you can't pick yet are shaded. If you tap one, the app tells you why (covered on top, or blocked on both sides).

Frequently asked questions

Can every board be solved? Yes. Every board is built so it can be cleared, and shuffling keeps it solvable.

I'm stuck. What can I do? Use Undo as many times as you like, it's free. You can also use Hint (free hints every day) or Shuffle (once per board for free).

The tiles are hard to tell apart. Turn on Settings → Easy Read to show a large number or letter on every tile.

Will I lose my game if I close the app? No. Your board is saved and you can continue where you left off.

I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads. Open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it. Optional reward videos for extra hints stay available if you want them.

Will my progress come back if I reinstall the app or get a new phone? Yes, with no account needed. On iPhone, progress is kept in your iCloud (same Apple ID, iCloud turned on). On Android, it comes back through your Google backup when backup is turned on. A "Remove ads" purchase can always be restored from Settings → Restore purchase.
