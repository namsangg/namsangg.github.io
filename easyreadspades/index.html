Easy Read Spades & Hearts

Spades and Hearts against fair computer players, with big cards and your own pace. After every hand you can see all four hands and replay the same deal.

How to play

New to a game? From the home screen tap Learn Spades in 1 minute or Learn Hearts in 1 minute. You can also open How to play at any time, or tap the book icon during a game.

Spades: you and the player across from you are a team. Before each hand, bid how many tricks you expect to win. Everyone must follow the suit that was led; spades beat every other suit. Make your bid to score, miss it and you lose points.

Hearts: every player is alone. Avoid taking hearts (1 point each) and the Queen of Spades (13 points). The lowest score wins. Take all of them and you "shoot the moon".

Frequently asked questions

Does the computer see my cards? No. Computer players only use what they could see at a real table. After each hand, tap Show all hands to see every card that was dealt, and Replay this deal to play the same cards again.

Why did that player win the trick? A short line under the table explains each trick (for example, "highest club" or "spades beat other suits"). You can turn it off in Settings.

Our house plays different rules. Open House rules to choose the target score, Nil and Blind Nil, bag penalty, how shooting the moon scores, the Jack of Diamonds and more.

The cards move too fast. Choose a slower card speed in Settings. The result of each hand stays on screen until you tap to continue.

Will I lose my game if I close the app? No. Your game is saved and you can tap Continue on the home screen.

I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads. Open Settings → Remove ads and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it. Optional reward videos for extra hints stay available if you want them.

Will my progress come back if I reinstall the app or get a new phone? Yes, with no account needed. On iPhone, it is kept in your iCloud (same Apple ID, iCloud turned on). On Android, it comes back through your Google backup when backup is turned on.
