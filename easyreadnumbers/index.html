Easy Read Numbers: Match Ten

A classic number-matching puzzle with big, easy-to-read numbers, three board colors (Paper, Night and High contrast) and a free "Add numbers" button.

How to play

Tap two numbers that are the same (7 and 7) or that add up to 10 (3 and 7). They disappear. The two numbers can be side by side, above and below, or on a diagonal, as long as only empty spaces are between them. The last number of a row also connects to the first number of the next row.

When a row is empty it goes away. Clear every number to finish the board.

Frequently asked questions

I'm stuck. What do I do? Tap Add numbers. The numbers that are left are copied to the bottom of the board. It is always free and you can use it as often as you like. Undo is free too.

Can every board be cleared? Yes. Every board is made so it can be cleared without adding numbers. If you take a different path you may need to add numbers, undo a few moves, or start the board again.

The white screen is too bright. Choose Settings → Board colors → Night for a dark board, or High contrast for black on white.

I tapped two matching numbers but nothing happened. Something is in the way. Only empty spaces can be between the two numbers.

Will I lose my game if I close the app? No. Your board is saved and you can continue where you left off.

I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads. Open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it. Optional reward videos for extra hints stay available if you want them.

Will my progress come back if I reinstall the app or get a new phone? Yes, with no account needed. On iPhone, progress is kept in your iCloud (same Apple ID, iCloud turned on). On Android, it comes back through your Google backup when backup is turned on. A "Remove ads" purchase can always be restored from Settings → Restore purchase.
